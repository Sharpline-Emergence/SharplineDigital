A tidier way to read reliable Canadian news.

North Wire is a Canadian-curated news reader we've been building — updated daily, no duplicate stories, no ads on our end. It's live and free to use right now, and we'd love your honest take.

Try North Wire → Opens in a new tab · works great on iPhone, just add it to your home screen

What makes it different

✓

Updated daily

Fresh headlines pulled automatically every morning — no stale feed.

✓

Reliable Canadian sources

CBC, Global News, Financial Post, and other established outlets — not aggregator spam.

✓

Few duplicate stories

We filter out near-identical headlines from different outlets, so you're not reading the same story three times.

✓

No ads on our end

Just headlines, summaries, and a link to the original source. Nothing cluttering the feed.

Heads up — this is an active beta. You might hit the occasional rough edge as we keep improving it. It's free to use for now, and being part of the beta means you're helping shape what it becomes.
