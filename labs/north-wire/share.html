North Wire is a Canadian-curated news reader built to cut through the noise. It pulls headlines from trusted sources — CBC, CTV, Global News, Financial Post, BBC — with no account required, no cookie tracking, and no algorithm chasing your attention. You can add it straight to your phone's home screen from your browser, no app store needed. It's currently in public beta, with feedback welcomed right in the app. news.sharplinedigital.com
